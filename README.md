# Face Off

A two-player emoji game for starting conversations, sharing memories, and having fun.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser, enter both players' names, choose a level, then press **Start** and **Stop** to reveal an emoji and a prompt. Players can imitate the emoji or respond to the prompt. Press **Next round** to continue.

## Levels

- **Icebreakers:** Easy, upbeat conversation starters.
- **Funny & Silly:** Playful questions and funny memories.
- **Friendship Memories:** Favorite moments and things you appreciate about each other.
- **Deep & Meaningful:** Thoughtful questions about feelings, support, and hopes.
- **Wildcard:** Surprise questions and optional mini-challenges.

Choose any level at any time. Changing levels keeps the round counter and starts that level's prompts over.
